Guide for physician assistants

You keep the clinic ready

You set patients up, make sure everyone has agreed, prepare each visit and tidy what families send in. When you do this well, the doctor spends visits deciding, not hunting for information.

1Your Today queue

Today is a work list of things that need a person. Do them top to bottom. When a task is finished it disappears.

2Onboard a patient

Press Onboard patient (sidebar). Four steps. Your progress is saved as a draft, so you can stop and come back.

  1. Patient.Use the number the patient has on WhatsApp. Two patients cannot share a number. Pick the treating doctor.
  2. Care circle.Add one or two people (maximum two). Level 1 is contacted first, Level 2 is the backup. For each give name, relationship and WhatsApp number. Keep dashboard access ticked if they should see the patient's page.
  3. Baseline.Profile, intake vitals, medical history, lifestyle, current medicines (from any doctor) and recent labs. This fills Visit 1 for the doctor and starts the lab trend lines. Baseline labs never raise alerts.
  4. Review and create.Check each card (every one has an Edit button). When you create the patient, welcome and consent messages go out on WhatsApp.
💾

Interrupted? Your drafts are listed on Today (Onboarding). Open one to resume at the step you left.

3Fill the baseline from old documents

Discharge summaries, old prescriptions and lab reports are in the baseline step as ✨ Fill from documents.

⚠️

You are the safety check. Read each field against the paper. The reader lists warnings for anything it was unsure of (blurred handwriting, two documents that disagree).

Medicines the documents show as stopped go to history, not to current medicines.

4Make sure everyone has agreed

After creation, the patient and each caregiver gets a WhatsApp message asking them to reply YES. Until they do, the patient shows under Onboarding and a Consent task appears.

🤖

CareCircle follows up first

It sends reminders on its own after a while.

📞

Then you

Press Call to phone them, or Resend to send the reminder again.

If a caregiver replies NO, the clinic is told and you get a task, so you can ask the family for someone else.

5Prepare a visit

Before a visit, press Prepare visit on the patient or from a visit task. This gives the doctor a head start.

  1. Look at what changed.The page shows the care plan, since-last-visit trends, alerts and reported changes from other doctors.
  2. Flag what matters.Press Flag for doctor next to a reported medicine change, or add your own flag (“skin rash since Tuesday”, “family wants to discuss diet”). Add the questions the family wants to ask.
  3. Mark ready.Press Mark ready for Dr …. The doctor sees “brief ready”. You can press Undo ready if something changes. The doctor can always edit.
🧠

The doctor's visit builder includes your flags and the family's questions, and answers them in the plan sent to the family.

6Documents and other doctors' changes

Documents the family sends

Photos and PDFs sent on WhatsApp wait on Today as Document to file until you press Review & file. Give each a proper title and category (prescription, lab report, discharge summary, imaging, other), add notes, and file it. Files you upload yourself are filed at once. Limit 5 MB each.

Changes reported by other doctors

When the family tells CareCircle that a specialist changed a medicine, you get a task. The reminders do not change on their own.

✋

Open the prescription photo and compare it with what was reported. Edit the dose or times if needed before pressing Apply. If a change looks risky or confusing, flag it for the doctor instead.

7Fix a care circle that is not responding

If a caregiver misses most alerts (three or more, over 60%, in two weeks), a Care circle task appears naming who and how many. Press Manage circle.

  • Check the phone number and that WhatsApp is working.
  • Ask the family whether someone else should be the contact.
  • Swap or replace a caregiver. A circle has at most two people.
