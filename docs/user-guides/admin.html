Guide for clinic admins

Set up the clinic once, then step back

The admin creates the clinic, adds doctors and assistants, and decides whether AI assistance is on. You do not see patient information, and you never need to.

  • 1Create the clinic
  • 2Add your team
  • 3AI assistance
  • 4Go live
  • 5Start afresh
  • 6Keeping it safe
🔐

The admin page holds no patient data. It manages the clinic and its staff only.

1Create the clinic

On your first sign-in you see “Set up your clinic”. Three boxes, one button.

2Add doctors and assistants

Below the clinic details, there are two lists: Doctors and Assistants.

🩺

Doctors

Each doctor sees only their own patients. They record visits and set alert limits.

🧑‍💼

Assistants (PAs)

Onboard patients and see every patient in the clinic. They prepare visits and file documents.

  1. Press “+ Add doctor” (or assistant).
  2. Fill in the form.Full name and mobile are required. Also: specialty or designation, email, and for doctors the medical registration number (for example KMC 123456).
  3. Press “Add doctor”.They can now sign in. Use Edit to change details, or Remove (you will be asked to confirm). You cannot remove yourself.
⚠️

A doctor who is still the treating doctor for patients cannot be removed. Reassign those patients first (a doctor or assistant can do this from each patient's profile).

3AI assistance (Gemini)

Press ✨ Configure Gemini and paste your API key. The page shows On with the model name, or Off · rule-based.

With AI onWith AI off
Understands free-text, voice notes and other Indian languages. Reads prescription and lab photos. Drafts the pre-visit brief and care-plan draft. Has natural conversations about other doctors' visits.Still works. Messages are read with fixed rules, and the assistant asks fixed questions in order. Photos are filed but not read.
📏

Alerts never depend on AI. Rules and the doctor's limits decide every alert. AI only reads and drafts. A person checks anything it drafts before it reaches a patient or the record.

Get a key from Google AI Studio. The key is stored in the clinic's database. Do not share it in chat or email.

4Go live: who does what

The first time, Today shows a “Getting started” checklist to your team. It ticks itself off.

Patientat home

5Start afresh

🧨

This erases everything.

The clinic, staff, patients, messages and records are all removed. It cannot be undone. To confirm you must type the clinic's exact name. It is meant for demos and fresh starts, never for a clinic in use.

6Keeping it safe

💾

Back up regularly

Whoever runs the server should take backups and test a restore. See the README: npm run clinic:backup.

🔑

Protect the site

Ask your technical contact to set a site password (APP_PASSWORD) before sharing the link outside the clinic.

👥

Keep staff current

Remove staff who leave. Check that every doctor has a medical registration number.

📣

Tell patients what to expect

Share the patient and caregiver guides at onboarding.

📌

This version uses a simple persona picker for sign-in and a WhatsApp simulator. Production sign-in and the real WhatsApp Business connection are planned. See docs/ROADMAP.md.
