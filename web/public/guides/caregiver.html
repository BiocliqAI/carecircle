Guide for caregivers

You are the safety net

You are in the patient's care circle: family or a trusted helper who gets a message when something needs attention. You are not on call all day. CareCircle tells you only when it matters, and tells you what to do.

🚨

If the patient is in danger, call 108.

Chest pain, struggling to breathe, fainting, new confusion. Call first, reply to CareCircle afterwards.

1Your role

A care circle has up to two people, in order. The order decides who is messaged first.

1️⃣

Primary caregiver

Messaged first when something needs attention. Takes ownership by replying ACK.

2️⃣

Backup caregiver

Messaged if the primary does not answer in time, or passes the alert on.

The patient's doctor and assistants are not paged. They watch a dashboard and review it. That is why your reply matters: you are the first human responder.

2Joining the care circle

📞

Save the CareCircle number in your phone as “CareCircle”, and make sure WhatsApp notifications are on. An alert you do not see cannot be answered.

3When an alert comes

There are three kinds. Each has a clock: if you do not answer, it moves on to the next person.

KindLooks likeNormal time to answer
🚨 UrgentChest pain, very high BP, very low sugar, help requestedabout 15 minutes
⚠️ Needs attentionA reading outside the doctor's limits, weight gain, a worrying lab resultabout 1 hour
📋 Missed taskMedicines or readings not reported for a whileabout 2 hours
⏳

A little above the limit is rechecked first. If the patient's BP or pulse is only slightly high, CareCircle asks them to rest and measure again, and alerts you if it is still high or no recheck comes within 30 minutes. Emergencies are never delayed. If a wrong number caused an alert, you will get a message that it was a correction and the alert is closed.

The doctor can set different times for each patient. Your alert message tells you why you were contacted and what the doctor advised.

4How to respond

  1. Reply ACK.This tells the others in the circle you have it. For health alerts, the patient is told you are following up.
  2. Call or check on the patient.Use the number in the message. Follow the doctor's advice written there.
  3. Tell CareCircle what happened.Reply 1, 2, 3 or 4 (see below). For 2, 3 and 4 add one short line.
🔁

After you close an alert about a reading, CareCircle asks the patient to measure again about two hours later (for weight, next morning) and tells you whether it settled. If it is still high you are alerted again, and if no recheck comes you are told so you can check in. You do not need to chase it yourself.

Cannot take it right now?

Reply Miss (or pass). The alert goes straight to your backup. No one is upset. It is far better than letting it sit.

✋

If the patient completes the missed task themselves, a “missed task” alert closes automatically.

5Log something for the patient

If you are with them, you can send readings, tablets and symptoms from your own phone. It is saved as logged by you.

Photos of the BP machine, sugar meter or scale work too: CareCircle reads the numbers and asks you to confirm. If you typed something wrong, send sorry, that was 128, not 182 and it is corrected.

On your page, use the box “Log something for Gopal” and press Log it. It does the same thing.

6Heads-ups and the evening summary

Sometimes you get “a heads-up, not an alarm”: CareCircle noticed a slow change (weight up three days running, BP drifting up, medicines being missed more often). Nothing has crossed a limit and there is nothing to ACK. Have a look at how they are doing. The doctor sees it too.

Each evening you get one short message so you do not have to wonder.

7Record visits to other doctors

You are often the one who goes with the patient to the specialist. After the visit, take a minute to tell CareCircle, so the primary doctor and the rest of the family see the same advice.

On WhatsApp

Just tell it, for example:

We took appa to the cardiologist and he reduced Lasix to half in the morning and added nifedipine 10 mg three times a day

It confirms what it understood, asks only what is missing (which doctor, the exact dose, a photo of the prescription, when the next visit is) and then thanks you. You can send the prescription photo at any point. Say stop to save what you have so far.

On your page

  1. Press “+ Add a visit”.Under “Other doctors”.
  2. Describe it, attach the prescription, press “Fill the form for me”.
  3. Check, then “Save visit”.Everyone in the care circle and the clinic can now see it.
⏳

Medicine reminders do not change until the doctor says so. Keep giving medicines as per the current plan until you see “Added to the plan” or are told on WhatsApp. If you are unsure, call the clinic.

8Your page

Open your CareCircle link and pick your name. Your WhatsApp is on the left. The patient's day is on the right.

9Good habits

⚡

Answer fast, even with “Miss”

A quick pass to your backup beats silence. Silence delays help.

📝

Write one useful line

“Clinic said keep fluids low” helps the doctor far more than “done”.

📷

Photograph prescriptions

After any doctor visit, photograph the prescription and send it right there in the chat.

🤝

Talk to your backup

Agree who is away when. If you travel, tell the clinic so the order can be changed.

💡

Remember: CareCircle does not replace a doctor. It never tells you to change a medicine. If you are worried, call the clinic or 108.
