Guide for doctors

See what changed, not every message

CareCircle watches the weeks between visits so you do not have to. Families handle the alerts. You review a short list, and at each visit you start from what actually happened at home.

1How it works

🏠Patient at homelogs on WhatsApp

🔕

You are never paged

CareCircle does not message doctors or assistants. Urgent matters are for the family and emergency services.

📏

Rules decide alerts

Your limits trigger alerts, not AI. AI only reads messages, photos and drafts summaries for you to check.

✍️

You own the plan

Medicines and limits change only when you (or your assistant, on your behalf) change them.

🔒

You see your own patients. Your assistants see every patient in the clinic.

2Your Today

After you sign in you land on Today. It is a short list of patients who need you, most urgent first, with one reason each.

4Record a visit

Press Start visit. The visit builder turns your consultation into a care plan in three steps. You check every line before it reaches the patient.

  1. Capture.Add the Prescription photo, press Listen to consultation while you talk with the patient and family, and/or Dictate your advice. Then press Build draft. If your assistant prepared the visit, their flags and the family's questions are included.
  2. Review the draft.Medicines are marked new, changed, same or stopped. Each line says where it came from (prescription, conversation, dictation, or carried over). Unclear numbers are highlighted. If the prescription and your spoken words disagree, you choose which is right, and conflicts must be resolved before sending. A taper (“40 mg for 2 weeks, then 20 mg”) becomes two steps automatically.
  3. Send.You see the exact WhatsApp message the patient will receive: medicines with timings, what to send, limits, next visit, and answers to the family's questions. Confirm, and reminders start. The care circle is told that the plan was updated.
🧐

AI drafts, you decide. The builder never invents medicines, doses or limits. It quotes the sentence it relied on. If something is missing from your sources, it is left out, not guessed.

The first visit is pre-filled from the baseline your assistant collected: current medicines, intake vitals and allergies. A simpler manual form is also available, where you type the plan field by field.

5“In short” for the family, and the next visit

Step 3 of the visit builder also drafts a few plain lines for the family. You check them before anything is sent.

One to two days before a visit, the AI brief is also prepared automatically. It waits on the chart (marked “prepared automatically”) and on your assistant's Prepare visit page.

6Alert limits and timers

Part of each plan. When a reading crosses a limit, the care circle is alerted, not you. Defaults below; change them per patient in the visit.

ReadingDefault alert
Blood pressureabove 150 / 95, or systolic below 100
Pulseabove 110 or below 50
Blood sugarabove 250 or below 70 mg/dL
Oxygen (SpO₂)below 92%
Weightgain of 2 kg within 3 days (kidney and heart-failure plans use a dry-weight band)
Pain score7 or more

⏱ Urgent

Caregiver has 15 min before the next level is called.

⏱ Deviation

60 min for a reading outside limits.

⏱ Missed task

120 min for medicines or readings not reported.

🛟

Emergency rules always apply, even before Visit 1: BP 180/110 or higher, oxygen below 88%, sugar below 54, chest pain, fainting, or a help request. They alert the care circle straight away.

7Smarter alerts: rechecks, patterns, symptom answers

Your limits still decide every alert. These features make sure the family is alerted for real problems and early drift, not typos.

⏳

Recheck first

A borderline high BP (up to 20 mmHg systolic and 10 diastolic over your limit) or pulse (up to 20 bpm over) is not alerted at once. The sender is asked to rest 10 minutes and measure again. The family is alerted if it is still high or no recheck comes in 30 minutes. Emergency rules and larger deviations alert immediately.

🔁

Closing the loop

When a caregiver closes a reading-based alert, the patient is asked to measure again about two hours later (weight: next morning) and the caregiver hears whether it settled. Still high alerts again, with no delay.

✏️

Typos and corrections

Impossible values (weight 592, BP 1400/80) are not saved; the sender is asked “did you mean…?”. “Sorry, that was 128, not 182” replaces the earlier reading, re-runs the rules and closes an alert the typo caused. The old value stays in the audit trail.

📷

Photos of machines

A photo of a BP monitor, sugar meter, scale, oximeter or thermometer is read and logged once the sender confirms. These never reach your assistant's queue.

Patterns: slow drift under your limits

Four patterns are watched each morning and after each reading: weight up three days in a row (by at least 0.9 kg in all), the average top BP number up 10 or more on the week before while still under your limit, weight up 1 kg in two days together with swelling, breathlessness or a missed water tablet, and medicines confirmed on under 70% of doses after a good week. They are advisory: the patient and first caregiver get one gentle “heads-up, not an alarm”, and you see a blue Pattern strip and the reason on Today.

8Ask the record, and see what changed

At the top of the Overview tab, ask a question about this patient in your own words.

The same markers are on the vitals and lab charts. Under Vitals & labs every medicine change, including changes by other doctors, is an amber line. Several changes on one day are one line with all of them in its tooltip. You can see at a glance what happened to BP, weight or creatinine after each change.

9When the patient sees another doctor

Specialists change medicines between your visits. Families can record these visits on WhatsApp or on their page. You see them as a strip on the chart and as a panel under Visits & notes.

Amber checks beside each change

Fixed rules, not AI, flag what is worth a second look before you or your assistant press Apply. Nothing is blocked.

  • The same type of medicine is already in the plan (for example two beta blockers), or the same drug under another brand.
  • An allergy on file names the drug, a brand of it, or another drug of the same type.
  • Potassium of 5.0 or more with an ACE inhibitor, ARB, ARNI or potassium-sparing water tablet.
  • Reduced kidney function (eGFR under 30 or creatinine 2.5 or more) with metformin, an anti-inflammatory painkiller or a potassium-sparing water tablet.
  • An anti-inflammatory painkiller with a blood thinner, or in heart failure or kidney disease.
  • Recent low BP before another BP-lowering medicine.
  • A dose that more than doubles or halves.
  • Stopping a blood thinner in atrial fibrillation, or an antiplatelet in heart disease.
👀

If a change looks unsafe, do not apply it. Reach the family yourself. When several changes arrive in one visit, Apply all handles them together. Stopping a medicine that is not in the plan will say so.

10Notes, documents and summaries

📝

Clinical notes

Under Visits & notes. Visible to the care team only, never to the patient or family. Type or press Dictate; dictated text is added to the box so you can check it before saving. You can only edit notes you wrote; every change is audited.

📄

Documents

Photos, PDFs and scans up to 5 MB, by category (prescription, lab, discharge, imaging, voice, other). “Read a prescription or report with AI” pulls out values for you to confirm.

✨

AI summary

One screen on Overview that explains what changed since the last visit, in plain sentences, with points to discuss. It is prepared automatically 1 to 2 days before a visit, and Refresh makes a new one. A draft for you, built from the record.

⇄

Compare visits

Side-by-side view of two visits: plan, vitals and what changed, so you can see what worked.

11Good to know

I am not getting WhatsApp alerts.

That is by design. Alerts go to the family. You see the same events on Today, under the patient's alerts, and in the activity feed.

Can my assistant do things for me?

Your assistants onboard patients, collect the baseline, prepare visits, file documents and chase consent. They can also apply or note other doctors' changes. You are still the one who records visits and sets limits. See the assistant's guide.

A caregiver declined or stopped answering.

Your assistant gets a “Care circle not responding” task and can update the care circle (maximum two people: a primary and a backup).
