How it fits together

Three rules everyone should know

🚨

Emergency? Call 108.

CareCircle is not an emergency service and nobody reads messages in real time.

📏

The doctor sets the rules

Limits and medicines change only when the doctor (or their assistant) changes them. A family message never changes a reminder by itself.

🤝

Consent comes first

Patients and caregivers reply YES before anything is shared. NO is always allowed.

What CareCircle does for you

The smart parts work quietly in the background. Each guide shows them with real examples.

🤔

Catches typos

“Weight 592?” Did you mean 59.2? Wrong number? Say “sorry, that was 128”.

 📷

Reads your machine

Photograph the BP monitor, sugar meter or scale; confirm; done.

 ⏳

Checks twice before alerting

A slightly high reading is rechecked first, and after an alert closes it follows up.

 💬

Asks the right follow-up

Two quick questions after a symptom, saved for the doctor.

 👀

Spots slow drift

Weight, BP or adherence creeping the wrong way, before any limit is crossed.

 🌐

Speaks your language

Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Bengali, Gujarati, Punjabi.

 🔎

Answers from the record

“When did creatinine start rising?” with the chart and medicine changes.

 📄

Reads documents on arrival

One tap to file a lab report, with checks on other doctors' changes.

 📝

Writes the “In short”

A plain-language summary for the family and a suggested next visit.
🤝

Rules and the doctor's limits decide every alert. The AI reads, drafts and points; a person confirms anything clinical. Without AI the clinic keeps working.

Words used in the guides

Care circle

The one or two people (primary and backup) who get alerts for a patient.

Care plan

The medicines, readings, limits and advice the doctor sets at a visit. Reminders follow it.

Alert

A message to the care circle when a reading is outside the limits, a task is missed, or help is asked for.

ACK

A caregiver's reply that means “I have this”. It stops the clock and tells the others.

Baseline

The patient's starting information (history, medicines, labs) collected at onboarding.

Other doctors

Specialists, hospitals or a local GP. Their visits and changes are recorded here and reviewed by the primary doctor.
