Guide for patients

Your care, between visits

CareCircle reminds you about medicines and checks, keeps a record for your doctor, and lets your family step in if something looks wrong. You do almost everything by chatting on WhatsApp, the way you already do.

🚨

In an emergency, call 108.

Chest pain, trouble breathing, fainting or confusion: call first. CareCircle is not an ambulance service and nobody reads your messages in real time.

1Say hello

Your clinic adds you to CareCircle. You will get a welcome message on WhatsApp.

Your care circle is the one or two family members you chose, a first contact and a backup. They get a message too and can reply YES to join.

💡

If you would rather not share, reply NO. Nothing is shared. You can change your mind later by replying YES or telling the clinic.

2Your day with CareCircle

At the times your doctor chose, CareCircle sends one short message listing what is due. You reply once, in your own words.

💊

Medicines

Tell it when you have taken them, or when you missed them.

📏

Readings

BP, weight, sugar, pulse, oxygen: whatever your doctor asked for.

📝

How you feel

A daily check on swelling, breathlessness, pain, or “all fine”.

Hindi, Tamil and mixed languages work too, for example “dawa le li” or “potachu” for “tablets taken”.

Made a mistake? Just say so

Send sorry, that was 128, not 182 or typo, BP should be 128/82. CareCircle fixes the earlier reading. If the wrong number had alerted your family, the alert is closed and they are told.

If you type an impossible number like weight 592, it is not saved. CareCircle asks “Did you mean 59.2 kg?” and you tap Yes.

If it cannot tell what you meant

A lone number like “96” could be weight, sugar or pulse. CareCircle asks, and you tap the right button. It never guesses silently.

4If a reading is off

Your doctor sets safe limits for you. If a reading goes outside them, this is what happens.

📱You send a readinge.g. BP 168/102
→outside your limits
💬You get advicerest, recheck, when to seek care
→same moment
👪Your family is toldfirst contact, then backup
⏳

A reading only a little too high is checked again first. You will be asked to rest for 10 minutes, then measure again. If it is still high, or you do not send another, your family is told. A serious reading is never delayed.

You are not left to handle it alone, and you are not alarmed for nothing: your caregiver gets a message that explains why, and takes ownership by replying ACK.

ℹ️

Your doctor is not paged. Doctors and assistants see your readings and alerts on their screen and review them. Do not wait for the clinic to call if you feel unwell. Call them, or 108 in an emergency.

If you forget to reply for a while, your caregiver may get a gentle nudge too. That is normal, not a problem.

5Photos, reports and voice notes

  • Send a photo or PDF of a prescription, lab report or discharge summary with the 📎 button. It is saved in your record for the care team. File size limit: 5 MB.
  • Photograph your BP machine, sugar meter or scale. CareCircle reads the numbers and asks “I read this as BP 138/86. Is that right?” Tap Yes, log it. Make sure the numbers are sharp and fill the photo.
  • Send a voice note with the microphone when typing is hard. It is turned into text so your readings are still picked up, and the recording is kept for your care team.

6In an emergency

🆘

Call 108 first.

Then press SOS in your WhatsApp chat (or send HELP). Your care circle is alerted straight away and your primary caregiver is called.

7Seen another doctor? Tell CareCircle

A cardiologist, a hospital, the local GP. Your doctor and family should know what they changed, so nothing is missed or doubled.

On WhatsApp, in your own words

Or on your page

  1. Open “Other doctors” and press “+ Add a visit”.You will find it on your page, below next visit.
  2. Write what happened, add a photo, press “Fill the form for me”.Or press “I'll fill it myself”.
  3. Check the form and press “Save visit”.Doctor, date, medicine changes, next visit. Fix anything that is wrong.
⏳

Your reminders do not change straight away. Your own doctor checks the change first. Until then the card shows “Waiting for Dr. …”. Once they add it to your plan, it says “Added to the plan” and you are told on WhatsApp.

The evening before the next visit you get a reminder. That evening CareCircle asks how it went.

Never stop or change a medicine because of this guide or a chat message. Follow your doctors' advice.

8Your page

Open your CareCircle link and choose your name. You will see your WhatsApp chat on the left and your page on the right. The page shows the same record your doctor sees.

9Questions people ask

Does my doctor read every message?

No. They see a summary and anything flagged, when they review your record. That is why you should call the clinic or 108 if you feel unwell.

Who can see my information?

Your care team (your doctor and the clinic's assistants) and the family members in your care circle. Each person joined only after replying YES.

I sent a wrong number.

Send the right one straight away, for example BP 132/84, that earlier one was wrong. If it matters, tell the clinic at your next visit.

I do not want these messages any more.

Tell the clinic. They will update your care circle and plan.

Can I use it in my language?

Yes. Write in your own language or mix words. The voice note button also understands Indian languages.
